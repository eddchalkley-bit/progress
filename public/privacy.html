Privacy policy

Draft · last updated 22 October 2026

Progress is a pacing and recovery diary. It helps you understand your own energy, sleep and symptoms. This page explains what it reads, why, and what happens to it.

What Progress reads

With your permission, Progress reads from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android):

  • sleep, including sleep stages and time awake
  • heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • steps, active energy and exercise time
  • breathing rate and blood oxygen

It also stores the check-ins you enter: symptom ratings, screen time, social and mental load, stress, rest breaks, eating and notes.

Progress never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Why

Only to show you your own patterns: your normal, your recovery, your energy budget, your sleep need, and what tends to come before a crash. Your data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Where it is stored

Your readings and check-ins are stored in a secure database (Supabase) and processed by the app’s server (Vercel). Access needs your private token. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Your choices

  • You can stop Progress reading health data at any time. On iPhone: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Progress. On Android: Health Connect → App permissions → Progress.
  • You can export your data as a spreadsheet from Settings in the app.
  • You can ask for all of your data to be deleted by emailing the address below.

Not medical advice

Progress is a self-management diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to your GP or care team about your health.
